Long-form clipper

turn one video into many shorts
Clip a video

Turns a long-form video into short clips — talk-heavy sources get hook-scored dialogue clips with captions; gameplay/action sources with little speech automatically switch to audio-peak highlight detection with punch-in zooms and impact text instead. YouTube/Twitch/Kick/ TikTok/Instagram/Reddit URLs are downloaded via yt-dlp (publicly accessible content is permitted; the platform's own export is preferred when available). ⚠ Action-mode clips have no original commentary — YouTube's reused-content policy requires real added commentary/value to monetize compiled footage, so add your own voiceover or on-screen take before publishing these.

Requires VIMEO_ACCESS_TOKEN — see .env.example
